02

THE EXTRACTION TRAIN

UMAE + SUPERCRITICAL CO2

A six-stage process line designed around dual-energy extraction — ultrasonic cavitation and microwave dielectric heating acting on biomass simultaneously — feeding a supercritical CO2 stage and a full downstream refinement train.

01

MILLING & PREP

Feedstock milled to a controlled particle size — the variable that decides how much surface the solvent and energy field can reach. Moisture content recorded per batch; inconsistent prep is where most extractions lose yield before they start.

02

UMAE REACTOR

Ultrasonic-microwave assisted extraction — cavitation ruptures cell walls while dielectric heating drives solvent into the matrix at once. ~32% higher yield than maceration or Soxhlet at 4–10× the speed, solvent-flexible across ethanol, water, hydrotropic and hydrocarbon systems.

03

SUPERCRITICAL CO2

The solvent-free tier — CO2 held above its critical point acts as a tunable solvent, selective for target compounds by pressure and temperature profile. No organic residue, the clean-label standard for food, cosmetic and supplement output.

04

WINTERIZATION

Cold ethanol precipitation strips waxes and lipids from crude — the step between "extract" and "oil." Recovery train reclaims ~95% of solvent, which is what keeps refinement economical at toll rates.

05

WIPED-FILM DISTILLATION

Short-path wiped-film evaporator fractionates refined oil by boiling point under high vacuum — seconds of heat exposure instead of hours, so thermolabile compounds survive the pass. Distillate exits at potency grade.

06

CHROMATOGRAPHY & CRYSTALLIZATION

Flash and preparative chromatography separate target compounds from co-extractives; controlled crystallization finishes select compounds at 99%+ isolate purity — the specification pharmaceutical and premium-ingredient buyers require.
