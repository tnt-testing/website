TEST SCOPE

METHOD-REFERENCED
IMPURITY

ELEMENTAL

USP <232>/<233> — the compendial elemental-impurity limits driven by ICH Q3D, the international guideline for metals in drug products. Cd, Pb, As, Hg, Co, V, Ni and full option-1 panels run by ICP-MS after validated digestion, reported against permitted daily-exposure limits.

SOLVENT

RESIDUAL

USP <467> residual solvents — Class 1–3 compendial limits measured by headspace GC-MS with Procedure A screening and confirmatory runs, covering the solvent list regulators audit on API and excipient lots.

ASSAY

API & DEGRADANTS

Active-ingredient potency plus related-substance and degradant panels by LC-MS/MS — the release and stability numbers a batch record needs. Unknown peaks escalate to the Orbitrap for formula-proposal identification.

MICRO

NONSTERILE LIMITS

USP <61>/<62> — microbial enumeration (total aerobic count, yeast & mold) and objectionable-organism testing for oral and topical products, the nonsterile release panel.

ROUTE

STERILE SCOPE

Endotoxin (LAL), particulate matter, container-closure integrity and sterility testing for injectable release — scheduled through the Regulated & Specialty department and quoted per protocol at intake.
