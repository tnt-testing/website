01

TEST SCOPE

METHOD-REFERENCED
METALS

RCRA-8 & FULL PANELS

The eight federally regulated metals — As, Ba, Cd, Cr, Pb, Hg, Se, Ag — used for hazardous-waste characterization, plus extended screens. Samples are microwave-digested per EPA 3051 and quantified by ICP-MS per 6020, with reporting limits below action levels.

ORGANICS

PAH / TPH / SOLVENTS

Polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons — combustion-derived contaminants common on industrial and fill sites — plus TPH split by carbon range (gasoline, diesel and oil-range organics) and solvent residues by GC-MS.

RESIDUES

PESTICIDES

Legacy and current-use residue panels by LC-MS/MS and GC-MS — organochlorines, organophosphates and modern actives for agricultural parcels and redevelopment sites.

AG

RUNOFF NUTRIENTS

Nitrate, phosphate and micronutrient-adjacent panels for soil fertility and agricultural-runoff assessment — the nutrient side of the environmental picture.
