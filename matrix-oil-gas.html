TEST SCOPE

METHOD-REFERENCED
NTA

IMPURITY PROFILE

Non-target analysis — high-resolution screening that identifies every detectable compound, not just a target list. The Orbitrap acquires at 280,000 FWHM with <3 ppm mass error, producing formula proposals for sulfur/nitrogen species, naphthenic acids and degradation products — reported as an annotated impurity map with confidence levels.

METALS

Ni · V · As

Nickel, vanadium and arsenic — the refinery-relevant trace metals that foul catalysts and drive blending decisions — quantified by ICP-MS in crude, condensate and process fluids after matrix-appropriate prep.

VOCs

BTEX / TPH

Benzene, toluene, ethylbenzene, xylenes and total petroleum hydrocarbons — the standard contamination metrics for stream quality and release assessment, by headspace GC-MS.

WATER

PRODUCED WATER

Formation water co-produced with hydrocarbons — high-TDS metals and organics panels under dilution-validated methods for disposal, reuse and compliance decisions.

02

SAMPLE REQUIREMENTS

CHAIN-OF-CUSTODY AVAILABLE
QTY

AMOUNT

≥ 50 mL representative of the stream. For non-target workups a field or trip blank is strongly recommended — it separates stream signal from transport contamination, which is where these analyses live or die.

PACK

CONTAINER

Sealed glass or steel only — no plastics for profiling work, since polymer leachables would land directly in the non-target spectrum. Hazardous-material shipping applies; coordinate classification before sending.

NOTE

FIELD LIMITS

Pressure-sensitive or time-critical field measurements (H2S, flash point at the wellhead) aren't lab scope — we'll tell you at intake rather than accept a sample we can't report on.
