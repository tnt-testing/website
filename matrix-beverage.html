TEST SCOPE

METHOD-REFERENCED
PROFILE

ACIDS & SWEETENERS

Organic acids (citric, malic, tartaric, phosphoric), caffeine, sweeteners and preservatives profiled by LC — the formulation-verification panel that confirms a batch matches its spec sheet. Also catches authenticity issues like acid-profile drift in juice products.

PRESERV

SORBATE / BENZOATE

Preservative assay quantifies sorbate, benzoate and sulfite levels against regulatory and label limits — the numbers that decide whether a low-pH product holds its shelf life.

METALS

TRACE METALS

Source-water and finished-product metals by ICP-MS — iron, copper, arsenic and packaging-contact elements tracked for contamination, flavor defects and regulatory limits.

MICRO

SPOILAGE & QA SCREENS

Aerobic count, yeast & mold and acid-tolerant spoilage organisms — the shelf-stability screens that matter in low-pH products where pathogens can't grow but spoilage organisms can.
