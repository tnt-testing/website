01

ELEMENTAL & METALS

ICP-MS →

Inorganic quantitation from trace to percent level — the workhorse scope for water, soil, supplements and petroleum matrices.

SCOPE

WHAT WE RUN

Heavy-metal quantitation from trace to percent level — Ni, V, Hg, As, Pb, Cd and full multi-element panels — plus USP <232>/<233> elemental impurities for release lots, mineral and nutrient panels for labels, RCRA-8 soil characterization, and drinking / process / produced water. If a question ends in 'how much metal is in this?', it lands here.

METHODS

HOW IT'S MEASURED

Samples are microwave-digested (EPA 3051/3052 closed-vessel) then quantified on ICP-MS per EPA 200.8 / 6020(B) and USP <233>. Reporting limits run below action levels — sub-ppb in clean matrices — and every batch carries a method blank, duplicate and spike recovery: a known amount spiked into the sample to prove the method measures accurately in that matrix.

SEND

SAMPLE NEEDS

Solids ≥ 1 g or liquids ≥ 25 mL in a clean unbreakable container. Flag high-organic or HF-requiring matrices at intake — they change digestion chemistry. Hold times apply to water (28 days preserved for metals); ship same-day where possible.

02

VOLATILE ORGANICS

GC-MS →

Solvent and aroma-class chemistry — anything that evaporates gets resolved, identified and quantified here.

SCOPE

WHAT WE RUN

Anything that evaporates — USP <467> residual solvents Class 1–3, BTEX and total petroleum hydrocarbons, fragrance and terpene fingerprinting, VOC screening for water and product, and cosmetic purity volatiles. The department covers both regulated compendial solvents and open screening.

METHODS

HOW IT'S MEASURED

Headspace GC-MS — the sample is warmed in a sealed vial and the vapor phase is injected, so the matrix never touches the column. USP <467> runs Procedure A screening with confirmation; screening work quantifies to ppm-level reporting with MS-identified peaks, not just FID bumps.

SEND

SAMPLE NEEDS

≥ 5 g or 10 mL in a sealed volatile-safe container with headspace minimized — volatiles equilibrate into headspace air and a half-empty vial under-reads. Ship cool; note expected solvent classes at intake so the screen window is set correctly.

03

NONVOLATILE ORGANICS

LC-MS/MS →

The regulated-residue department — pesticides, PFAS, vitamins and actives at nanogram-per-litre sensitivity.

SCOPE

WHAT WE RUN

The regulated-residue department — pesticide and PFAS panels, vitamins and actives, sweeteners, preservatives, and trace organic quantitation down to ng/L. Multi-residue MRM methods cover dozens of compounds in a single run, each confirmed by two fragment transitions.

METHODS

HOW IT'S MEASURED

Triple-quadrupole LC-MS/MS in MRM mode — analytes are chromatographically separated, then quantified against calibration with isotope-dilution where labeled analogs exist. LOQs reach ng/L for water-class matrices; each analyte carries its own validated limit of quantitation — the lowest level we report a defensible number at.

SEND

SAMPLE NEEDS

≥ 10 g or 25 mL; amber or foil-wrapped for light-sensitive analytes. PFAS samples: no PTFE contact anywhere in the chain — it leaches the exact compounds being measured.

04

UNKNOWN IDENTIFICATION

ORBITRAP →

When the question is 'what is this?' — high-resolution exact-mass screening with confidence-level reporting.

SCOPE

WHAT WE RUN

Non-target high-resolution screening — 'what is this?' work. Degradation products, unknown contaminants, extractables & leachables from packaging, foreign-material identification and failure analysis. Every detectable feature is reported, not just a target list.

METHODS

HOW IT'S MEASURED

Orbitrap acquisition at 280,000 FWHM resolving power with HCD fragmentation and <3 ppm mass error — enough to propose elemental formulas for unknown peaks. Identifications are reported with stated confidence levels (formula → structure → confirmed standard), not absolute claims.

SEND

SAMPLE NEEDS

A representative affected sample plus a control where possible — the comparison is what makes non-target work decisive. A photo of the defect or anomaly helps scope the workup before anything ships.

05

MICROBIOLOGY

NONSTERILE SCOPE

Nonsterile microbial limits, spoilage organisms and preservative performance for consumer-product matrices.

SCOPE

WHAT WE RUN

Nonsterile microbial limits — aerobic plate count, yeast & mold, and specified-organism screens (E. coli, Salmonella, S. aureus, P. aeruginosa, C. albicans) — plus preservative-effectiveness testing and BAM-aligned food-matrix procedures. Sterile scope (sterility, endotoxin) is covered under Regulated & Specialty below.

METHODS

HOW IT'S MEASURED

USP <61>/<62> enumeration and specified-organism procedures; USP <51> preservative challenge runs the full 28-day inoculation schedule. Counts are growth-based — the organism dictates the schedule, which is why micro TAT can't be compressed.

SEND

SAMPLE NEEDS

≥ 50 g / 50 mL sealed aseptically — micro samples need an unopened aliquot, separate from anything opened for chemistry. Ship same-day cool; plating starts on receipt and hold time is short.

06

NUTRITION & ALLERGEN

AOAC-REFERENCED

Label-claim substantiation chemistry — proximates, allergens and the measurements regulators actually ask for.

SCOPE

WHAT WE RUN

Label-claim substantiation — protein by combustion, fat by extraction, dietary fiber, sugars and organic acids, moisture and ash, plus gluten and major-allergen ELISA screens and water activity. The proximate package that backs Nutrition and Supplement Facts panels.

METHODS

HOW IT'S MEASURED

AOAC-referenced consensus methods per analyte — the validated procedures regulators and retailers accept for label claims. Allergen screens report at ppm sensitivity against kit thresholds; results are formatted for label-substantiation files.

SEND

SAMPLE NEEDS

≥ 100 g homogenized and representative of the lot; sealed retail packaging acceptable for finished goods — we sample across the package so the number represents the unit, not a scoop.

07

PHYSICAL & STABILITY

ICH-CONDITIONED

How a product behaves over time — conditioned storage, physical measurements and shelf-life documentation.

SCOPE

WHAT WE RUN

How a product behaves over time — ICH-conditioned stability storage, physical measurements and shelf-life documentation. pH, viscosity, moisture, texture and color tracked across pull points, plus package-integrity checks for the container side of shelf life.

METHODS

HOW IT'S MEASURED

ICH Q1A(R2) storage conditions — 25 °C/60 %RH long-term, 30 °C/65 %RH intermediate, 40 °C/75 %RH accelerated — with instrument SOPs per measurement. Pull schedules are fixed at kickoff so every timepoint has defined methods and acceptance criteria.

SEND

SAMPLE NEEDS

Enough units for every pull point — typically 3× the number of scheduled timepoints plus reserve. Stability studies are scheduled at kickoff; a missing mid-study unit can't be back-filled.

08

REGULATED & SPECIALTY

FULL-SERVICE COORDINATION

Sterile pharmaceutical scope, clinical and safety studies, and forensic/specialty analyses — scheduled as extended workups.

SCOPE

SPECIALTY SCOPE

Sterile pharmaceutical testing — sterility, bacterial endotoxin (LAL), particulate matter and container-closure integrity — plus SPF and clinical safety studies, flushability and disintegration, biocompatibility, and forensic & specialty analyses. Each workup is scoped and scheduled at intake against the governing protocol.
