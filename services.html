01

ELEMENTAL & METALS

ICP-MS →

Inorganic quantitation from trace to percent level — the workhorse scope for water, soil, supplements and petroleum matrices.

SCOPE

WHAT WE RUN

Heavy metals (Ni, V, Hg, As, Pb, Cd and full panels), elemental impurities for release lots, mineral & nutrient panels, RCRA-8 soil screening, drinking / process / produced water.

METHODS

REFERENCES

USP <232>/<233>, EPA 200.8, EPA 6020(B), microwave digestion per EPA 3051/3052.

SEND

SAMPLE NEEDS

Solids ≥ 1 g, liquids ≥ 25 mL, clean unbreakable container. Flag high-organic or HF-requiring matrices at intake.

02

VOLATILE ORGANICS

GC-MS →

Solvent and aroma-class chemistry — anything that evaporates gets resolved, identified and quantified here.

SCOPE

WHAT WE RUN

Residual solvents (USP <467> Class 1–3), BTEX / TPH, fragrance & terpene fingerprinting, VOC screening, cosmetic purity volatiles.

METHODS

REFERENCES

USP <467>, EPA 8260-style scope, HS-GC-FID screening with MS confirmation.

SEND

SAMPLE NEEDS

Sealed volatile-safe container (minimal headspace), ≥ 5 g / 10 mL. Ship cool; note expected solvent classes.

03

NONVOLATILE ORGANICS

LC-MS/MS →

The regulated-residue department — pesticides, PFAS, vitamins and actives at nanogram-per-litre sensitivity.

SCOPE

WHAT WE RUN

Pesticide & PFAS MRM panels, vitamins and actives, sweeteners, preservatives, regulated trace quantitation to ng/L.

METHODS

REFERENCES

Analyte-specific validated MRM methods; isotope-dilution quantitation where available.

SEND

SAMPLE NEEDS

≥ 10 g / 25 mL, amber or foil-wrapped for light-sensitive analytes (PFAS: no PTFE contact).

04

UNKNOWN IDENTIFICATION

ORBITRAP →

When the question is 'what is this?' — high-resolution exact-mass screening with confidence-level reporting.

SCOPE

WHAT WE RUN

Non-target HRAM screening, degradation-product and contaminant identification, extractables/leachables, foreign-material ID support, failure analysis.

METHODS

REFERENCES

280,000 FWHM exact-mass acquisition, HCD fragmentation, <3 ppm mass error formula proposals — identifications are reported with confidence levels, not absolute claims.

SEND

SAMPLE NEEDS

Representative affected + control sample preferred; photo of defect helps scope the workup.

05

MICROBIOLOGY

NONSTERILE SCOPE

Nonsterile microbial limits, spoilage organisms and preservative performance for consumer-product matrices.

SCOPE

WHAT WE RUN

Aerobic plate count, yeast & mold, specified-organism screens (E. coli, Salmonella, S. aureus, P. aeruginosa, C. albicans), preservative-effectiveness testing.

METHODS

REFERENCES

USP <61>/<62>, USP <51> PET, FDA BAM-aligned procedures for food matrices.

SEND

SAMPLE NEEDS

≥ 50 g / 50 mL sealed aseptically; ship same-day cool. Sterile scope (sterility, endotoxin) is covered under Regulated & Specialty — see below.

06

NUTRITION & ALLERGEN

AOAC-REFERENCED

Label-claim substantiation chemistry — proximates, allergens and the measurements regulators actually ask for.

SCOPE

WHAT WE RUN

Protein (combustion), fat, dietary fiber, sugars & organic acids, moisture/ash, gluten & allergen ELISA screens, water activity.

METHODS

REFERENCES

AOAC-referenced methods per analyte; label-claim verification packages for supplements and food.

SEND

SAMPLE NEEDS

≥ 100 g homogenized representative sample; retail packaging acceptable for finished goods.

07

PHYSICAL & STABILITY

ICH-CONDITIONED

How a product behaves over time — conditioned storage, physical measurements and shelf-life documentation.

SCOPE

WHAT WE RUN

ICH stability storage (25/60, 30/65, 40/75 conditions), pH, viscosity, moisture, texture & color measurement, package-integrity checks, shelf-life study support.

METHODS

REFERENCES

ICH Q1A(R2) storage conditions; instrument-specific SOPs per measurement.

SEND

SAMPLE NEEDS

Sufficient units per pull point (typically 3× timepoints); stability studies scheduled at kickoff.

08

REGULATED & SPECIALTY

FULL-SERVICE COORDINATION

Sterile pharmaceutical scope, clinical and safety studies, and forensic/specialty analyses — scheduled as extended workups.

SCOPE

SPECIALTY SCOPE

Sterile pharmaceutical testing (sterility, bacterial endotoxin, container-closure integrity), SPF & clinical safety studies, flushability/disintegration, biocompatibility, and forensic & specialty analyses — all scheduled as extended-scope workups through TNT.
