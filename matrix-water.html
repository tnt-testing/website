TEST SCOPE

METHOD-REFERENCED
METALS

TRACE METALS

EPA 200.8 / 6020(B) — the ICP-MS methods for metals in drinking, waste and groundwater. Full priority-metal scope plus minerals and hardness components, with reporting limits below action levels so results mean something at compliance margins.

VOCs

VOLATILE ORGANICS

BTEX and solvent-class volatiles by headspace GC-MS — the fuel and industrial-solvent signature panel for discharge, remediation and source-investigation work.

LC

PFAS & ORGANICS

Per- and polyfluoroalkyl 'forever chemicals' quantified at ng/L by isotope-dilution LC-MS/MS, plus nonvolatile contaminant panels for process and discharge water.

FIELD

HIGH-TDS & PRODUCED WATER

Oilfield and industrial brines don't run through drinking-water prep — high-TDS matrices get dilution-validated methods with matrix-matched calibration, flagged at intake and reported with dilution factors.

SAMPLE REQUIREMENTS

CHAIN-OF-CUSTODY AVAILABLE
QTY

AMOUNT

≥ 250 mL per analytical fraction — metals, VOCs and organics run as separate bottles because each has its own preservation chemistry and hold clock.

PACK

CONTAINER

Metals: acid-cleaned HDPE preserved to pH < 2. VOCs: 40 mL VOA vials filled zero-headspace. PFAS: HDPE only — no PTFE anywhere in contact.

SHIP

HANDLING

Cool to ≤ 6 °C and ship same-day — VOC hold times run 14 days from sampling under preservation, but every day in transit is a day off the analytical window. Chain-of-custody paperwork available for compliance and legal use.
