THE PROCESS

IONIZE → TRAP → IDENTIFY
STEP 01

UHPLC FRONT END

Vanquish UHPLC resolves the mixture by retention time; NanoAcquity handles low-flow trace work.

STEP 02

HESI-II IONIZATION

Heated electrospray nebulizes the eluent in N₂ — soft, intact ions ready for the trap.

STEP 03

ORBITRAP DETECTION

The C-trap injects ion packets into the Orbitrap; image-current decay yields 280,000 FWHM resolution across m/z 50–6,000.

STEP 04

HCD FRAGMENTATION

Higher-energy collisional dissociation maps daughter ions — the compound's structural fingerprint.

STEP 05

FORMULA ASSIGNMENT

Exact mass + isotope pattern + fragment match proposes the molecular formula to four decimal places.
