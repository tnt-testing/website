01

TEST SCOPE

METHOD-REFERENCED
METALS

ELEMENTAL IMPURITIES

Runs USP <232>/<233> — the compendial elemental-impurity limits for drug and supplement products. Cadmium, lead, arsenic, mercury plus expanded multi-element panels are digested in closed-vessel microwave systems and quantified by ICP-MS to sub-ppb reporting limits, with spike-recovery checks on every batch.

ACTIVES

ASSAY & LABEL-CLAIM

Confirms the potency printed on the Supplement Facts panel — vitamins, minerals and botanical actives quantified against reference standards by LC-MS/MS. Adulterant and undeclared-ingredient screening runs on the Orbitrap non-target platform, so unknown peaks get identified, not ignored.

SOLVENTS

RESIDUAL SOLVENTS

USP <467> covers solvents left over from extraction and manufacturing, classed 1–3 by toxicity. We screen all three classes by headspace GC-MS — ethanol extraction carryover, hexane residues and processing aids quantified against compendial limits.

MICRO

MICROBIAL LIMITS

USP <61>/<62> enumeration for nonsterile products — total aerobic microbial count, yeast & mold, plus specified organisms (E. coli, Salmonella, S. aureus, P. aeruginosa). USP <51> preservative-effectiveness challenge available where the formula calls for it.

NUTRITION

CLAIM CHEMISTRY

The proximate panel behind the Nutrition/Supplement Facts label — protein, fat, fiber, moisture and ash run by AOAC-referenced methods and reported per serving and per 100 g for label-substantiation files.

02

SAMPLE REQUIREMENTS

CHAIN-OF-CUSTODY AVAILABLE
QTY

AMOUNT

≥ 50 g composite or 3+ retail units per test group — enough for the method blank, duplicate and spike-recovery portions every batch requires. Oils and gummies change digestion setup, so flag them at intake and we'll quote the right prep.

PACK

CONTAINER

Sealed original packaging or a clean unbreakable container. Ordering micro scope too? Send a separate sealed unit — microbial limits need an unopened aliquot to stay defensible.

SHIP

HANDLING

Ambient shipping is fine for chemistry panels. Micro samples ship cool and same-day preferred — transit time eats the 24–72 hr hold window before plating must start.
