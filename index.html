INTAKE FAQ

SAMPLES · LOGISTICS · RESULTS

The five questions that cover most of a first submission. Anything else goes through the lab desk — response within one business hour.

Q1

HOW MUCH SAMPLE DO YOU NEED?

Matrix-dependent — typically 25–100 g or 250 mL. Each matrix page lists exact requirements; the lab confirms at intake.

Q2

HOW FAST ARE RESULTS?

Standard chemistry panels: 24–72 hr. Microbiology is growth-based: 3–5 days. In-house tests can rush under 24 hr for release-blocking decisions.

Q3

HOW DO I SHIP SAMPLES?

Sealed containers, cool shipment where flagged; container specs are on each matrix page. Chain-of-custody documentation available on request.

Q4

WHAT DOES A REPORT INCLUDE?

Method reference, results with uncertainty/confidence levels where applicable, and analyst sign-off. NDA available before sample discussion.

Q5

CAN YOU HANDLE WORK OUTSIDE THE STANDARD MENU?

Yes — specialty and regulated scope is quoted at intake. Full-service coordination means you never manage multiple laboratories.
