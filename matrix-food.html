01

TEST SCOPE

METHOD-REFERENCED
METALS

HEAVY METALS

FDA-aligned elemental panels for food — Pb, Cd, As, Hg and full multi-element screens. Samples are acid-digested and read on ICP-MS with reporting limits below the levels FDA guidance tracks for vulnerable products like infant food and juices.

RESIDUES

PESTICIDES & PFAS

Multi-residue MRM panels — one tandem-MS run quantifies dozens of pesticides, each compound confirmed by two fragment transitions. PFAS panels use isotope-dilution quantitation for ng/L-level reporting on produce, protein and packaging-contact matrices.

NUTRITION

PROXIMATES

The Nutrition Facts package — protein by combustion, fat by extraction, dietary fiber, moisture, ash, sugars and organic acids by AOAC-validated consensus methods, reported for label panels and spec sheets.

ALLERGEN

GLUTEN & ALLERGENS

ELISA-based detection of declared allergens — gluten, peanut, soy, milk, egg and tree nuts — at ppm sensitivity. Results report against kit thresholds for allergen-control verification and label decisions.

MICRO

FOOD MICRO & Aw

BAM-aligned (FDA's Bacteriological Analytical Manual) aerobic count, yeast & mold and pathogen screens. Water activity — the unbound water that predicts microbial shelf stability — measured alongside for stability modeling.

QTY

AMOUNT

≥ 100 g homogenized and representative of the lot — finished goods send 2–3 retail units so we can sample across the package. Homogeneity matters more than volume for residue work.

PACK

CONTAINER

Sealed food-grade bag or container. For PFAS scope: no PTFE-lined contact anywhere in the chain — it leaches the exact compounds we're measuring.

SHIP

Perishables ship cold with gel packs. Flag hold-time-sensitive analytes at intake — some residues and micro scopes carry 24–72 hr windows from sampling, not receipt.
