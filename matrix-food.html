TERMS & METHOD NOTES

PLAIN-LANGUAGE

The vocabulary on your quote and report — what each term means for your sample.

Multi-residue MRM
A tandem-MS screen that quantifies dozens of pesticides in a single run — each compound confirmed by two fragment transitions.
AOAC methods
Validated consensus procedures for nutrition and food chemistry — the reference behind label-claim panels.
Allergen / gluten testing
ELISA-based detection of declared allergens (peanut, soy, gluten) at ppm sensitivity.
Water activity (aw)
Unbound water in a product — the key predictor of microbial stability and shelf life.
BAM
FDA's Bacteriological Analytical Manual — the reference methods for food-pathogen testing.
