THE PROCESS

SEPARATE → FILTER → QUANT
STEP 01

UHPLC SEPARATION

The 1260 Infinity II binary pump drives C18 reversed-phase gradients up to 1,200 bar — compounds resolved by retention time before the MS ever sees them.

STEP 02

ELECTROSPRAY IONIZATION

The ESI source nebulizes eluent under heated N₂ flow — molecules are charged intact, positive and negative modes.

STEP 03

TRIPLE-QUAD MRM

Q1 selects the parent ion → the q2 collision cell fragments it → Q3 passes a signature daughter ion. Two transitions per analyte — near-zero false positives.

STEP 04

QUANT & REPORT

Peak areas ratio against certified reference standards and isotope-labelled internals — the number is generated, validated and reported.
