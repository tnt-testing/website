01

TEST SCOPE

METHOD-REFERENCED
METALS

HEAVY METALS

FDA cosmetic-guidance panels — lead, arsenic, mercury and cadmium, plus colorant-adjacent metals, run by ICP-MS to the levels regulators audit in color cosmetics and imported ingredients.

ACTIVES

ASSAY & ALLERGENS

Active concentrations (niacinamide, retinoids, acids) verified by LC-MS/MS and the EU-listed fragrance allergens profiled by GC-MS — the purity and sensitization data MoCRA safety-substantiation files need.

E&L

EXTRACTABLES & LEACHABLES

Compounds that migrate from packaging into the formula — screened by Orbitrap non-target identification so extractables get identified and reported, protecting purity claims on component changes.

MICRO

LIMITS + CHALLENGE

USP <61>/<62> microbial-limits testing for nonsterile cosmetics, plus the <51> preservative-effectiveness test — a 28-day inoculation study proving the formula's preservative system controls growth over its shelf life.

CLAIMS

CLINICAL & SPF STUDIES

SPF, broad-spectrum and safety-in-use testing scheduled as specialty studies — quoted per protocol at intake.

02

SAMPLE REQUIREMENTS

CHAIN-OF-CUSTODY AVAILABLE
QTY

AMOUNT

≥ 25 g / 25 mL per chemistry scope, plus a separate sealed unit for the micro aliquot — one opened container can't serve both scopes defensibly.

PACK

CONTAINER

Send the product in its original component where possible — for extractables/leachables relevance the packaging is part of the sample. Avoid decanting into PTFE containers.

SHIP

HANDLING

Ambient shipping is acceptable for most formulas; emulsions ship protected from freezing — a freeze-thaw cycle can break the emulsion before we ever plate it.
