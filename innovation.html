01

R&D PROGRAMS

SCOPED PER PROJECT

Discovery-stage work for companies that need a research lab's capability without building one — each program scoped at intake, run under NDA, documented to the standard your counsel or regulator will ask for.

ISOLATE

NOVEL COMPOUND ISOLATION

Extraction, fractionation and purification of target structures from botanical, fermentation or synthetic feedstock — taken from crude matrix to characterized isolate using the UMAE train and prep-scale chromatography. Yield and purity documented at every stage boundary.

CHAR

CHARACTERIZATION & STRUCTURE

High-resolution accurate-mass identification on the Orbitrap — formula proposals, fragmentation mapping and isotope-pattern confirmation — backed by quantitative purity profiling on LC-MS/MS and GC-MS. The analytical package that turns "we found something" into a documented structure.

METHOD

CUSTOM METHOD DEVELOPMENT

New methods developed and validated to the governing guideline — ICH Q2 parameters (specificity, linearity, accuracy, precision, LOD/LOQ) documented in a transfer-ready file, so the method survives handoff to your QA lab or a contract manufacturer.

DOSSIER

IP-SUPPORT DATA PACKAGES

Patent-grade characterization dossiers — spectra, fragmentation maps, purity profiles, stability data and method files assembled to prosecution standard for compounds, formulations and methods under development. The evidence layer between a discovery and a defensible claim.

FORM

FORMULATION & STABILITY R&D

Prototype formulation support with ICH-conditioned stability — delivery-system screening, excipient compatibility and shelf-life data that tells you whether the compound survives the product it has to live in.

02

THE PLATFORM

ANALYTICAL + EXTRACTION + BIO

Innovation programs draw on the full facility — the analytical stack that characterizes, the extraction train that isolates, and the in-vitro suite that handles biological feedstock and production-scale formats.

MS

ANALYTICAL STACK

Orbitrap HRAM for non-targeted identification (280K FWHM, <3 ppm mass error), LC-MS/MS for MRM quantitation, GC-MS for volatiles, ICP-MS for elemental — the same four platforms the testing departments run, applied to research questions.

UMAE

EXTRACTION TRAIN

The dual-energy UMAE reactor plus supercritical CO2, wiped-film distillation, flash/prep chromatography and crystallization — discovery-scale isolation that scales into toll production without changing platforms. Full detail on the extraction page.

BIO

IN-VITRO SUITE

Fed-batch fermenter for microbial and fermentation feedstock, flow cytometry for cell-line characterization, qPCR for molecular confirmation, tri-gas incubation, 96-well automated filling for plate-scale programs, and ULT storage — the biological layer for programs that go beyond chemistry.

03

THE IP MODEL

WORK-FOR-HIRE
YOURS

CLIENT OWNS THE IP

Programs are work-for-hire — compounds, methods, yields and characterization data belong to the customer, documented under NDA and delivered as a transferable dossier. We sell the capability; you keep the discovery.

PROOF

BUILT TO FILE

Ingredient companies productize IP — patented extracts, branded compounds, proprietary delivery systems. The dossier format is built for that path: spectra and fragmentation evidence counsel can cite, purity and stability data a filing can stand on, method files a licensee can run.

SCOPE

PROGRAM SHAPES

Single-analysis characterization (one compound, one dossier), staged development programs (isolate → characterize → formulate → stabilize), or standing R&D retainers for pipelines with recurring work. Every program is scoped and quoted at intake — no open-ended billing.
